What the research says

The options lean on a few consistent findings rather than pure trend-chasing:

  • Left-side vertical navigation is still the right pattern for a broad IA. It scales better than a top bar and supports scanning; keep labels visible, left-aligned and keyword-front-loaded. NN/g
  • Most mature SaaS products combine a sidebar for primary navigation with a top bar for global context — search, account, notifications and a primary create action. SaaSUI
  • Useful sidebar width is roughly 200–300px. Icons should support labels, not replace them; expandable groups and a responsive fallback matter as the IA grows. Navbar Gallery
  • Active state and grouping do most of the work. A clear “you are here” treatment, section labels and breathing room reduce scan time more than decoration does.
